Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

"A Musician's Life: Mozart, Chapter 1" (the "App") is made by Chen-Yu Yeh. The App does not collect any personal data.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login. The App never asks for your name, birthday, email, phone number, photos or location.
  • No ads, and no analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
